Services

What We Do (and What We Don't)

We keep our services tight so we can do them properly: battery-powered push mowing for small & medium lawns, plus a bit of small garden work on the side. We don't take on large lawns, ride-on jobs, or big landscaping projects.

Push Mowing

Regular battery-powered push mowing for small & medium residential lawns in Te Awamutu and Kihikihi. Mow, edges and a blow-down every visit — weekly, fortnightly, or a one-off tidy-up.

Small Hedges

Light trimming of small hedges to keep them neat alongside your mow — not large-scale hedge cutting or shaping jobs.

Weeding & Garden Tidy-Ups

A quick weed of garden beds and general tidy-up while we're already on site — small jobs, done well.
