What We Do

Small & Medium Lawns, Done Properly

We keep it simple: push mowing for small and medium-sized sections, plus a bit of garden tidy-up on the side. We don't take on large lawns or ride-on jobs — it lets us focus on doing the smaller jobs really well.

Push Mowing

Regular battery-powered mowing for small & medium residential lawns, with edges done properly every visit.

Small Hedges

Light hedge trimming to keep the front of your property looking sharp alongside your regular mow.

Weeding & Garden Tidy-Ups
