About Us

Locally Owned, Battery-Powered, and Proud of Every Lawn We Cut

Love Thy Lawn is run by Matt, mowing lawns around Te Awamutu and Kihikihi since 2021. It's a small, local operation — we deliberately stick to small and medium-sized lawns so every job gets proper attention, rather than being rushed through on a big round.

Every mower we run is 100% battery-powered. No two-stroke fumes, no petrol smell hanging over your section — just a clean, quiet cut. Alongside mowing, we do a bit of small garden work too: light hedge trims and weeding, done while we're already on site.

  • Locally owned and operated since 2021
  • 100% battery-powered equipment
  • Small & medium lawns only — no large properties
  • Serving Te Awamutu & Kihikihi
